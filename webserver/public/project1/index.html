I am fluid
complex, containing multitudes, brimming with feeling, alive.
This site is "me," rendered through my sensation .
the perspective turned into whatever my brain makes of the ordinary.
What I touch. What my eyes catch first.
What my ears pick up next. What stays in my nose.
What my mouth tastes.
All of it surfaces here — each piece scattered, each piece whole.
I feel it, I digest it, until a new sensation arrives, until it all dissolves into nothing.
There is only the fluid me here, endlessly being felt again.
