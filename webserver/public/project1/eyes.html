EYES

The world in front of me is scattered with starlight.
The street beneath my eyes is studded with little broken diamonds.
Manhattan glitters at night.
On my desk sits a stained-glass lamp, glowing amber.
This second, typing, I catch my own eyes staring back at me from the bottom of a yogurt spoon I just finished.
"Dusk is the hour my eyesight is worst"
a line from a play I can't help thinking of.
Every so often I check the chair by the dining table, to see if my cat bobby has fallen asleep there, then let my eyes linger, longingly, on the couch.
One of my eyes is nearsighted, the other farsighted.
so in class, when things are too far away, I always have to cover one eye just to see clearly.
I like sitting in the front row at the movies too.
Glasses, to me, are like a paid ad unlocking HD content
so I click skip, and go without them.
Everything I see: the green trees in the square, the dappled light, the curated aesthetics my phone keeps pushing at me, the shameless news back home, the way the world's current events show voices attacking women growing fewer
sand still, I see the sun rise again.
